# User Guide

Welcome to the AI Marketing Agent. This guide covers what you can
actually do in the app today — every feature described here is real
and working, not planned. For a technical audience, see the other
documents in `docs/`.

## Getting started

1. **Register** and create your business (your "organization").
2. **Complete onboarding** — a few questions about your business that
   let every AI feature (content, campaigns, the orchestrator) give
   you results tailored to your actual business, not generic output.
3. **Set your brand voice** under Business Profile — choose a preset
   (Professional, Friendly, Luxury, Educational, Funny, Bold,
   Inspirational) or write your own description. Every piece of AI-
   generated content uses this.

## Connecting your accounts

Go to **Integrations** to connect a social or advertising platform.
Only platforms your deployment has been configured to support will be
available — if a platform isn't listed, it hasn't been set up on this
installation yet, not a bug in your account.

## Creating and running a campaign

1. **Campaigns → New campaign.** Describe your product, your goal, and
   your budget.
2. **Generate.** The AI produces a real strategy, ad copy variants, and
   creative concepts based on your actual business profile.
3. **Approve.** Review what was generated before anything goes live —
   nothing publishes or spends automatically at this stage.
4. **Launch via Meta Ads.** Once you've connected a Meta Ads account
   (Integrations) and set a real daily spend limit, request the
   campaign go live. This creates a pending request, not an immediate
   change — a second, explicit confirmation is always required before
   any real spend happens. This is deliberate: the app is built so you
   always stay in control of your ad spend.

## Content

**Content → New** generates a single piece (a social post, product
description, blog post, etc.) from a short brief. **Content → Repurpose**
turns one piece into several platform-specific variants at once.
**Schedule** shows your content calendar — scheduled, draft, published,
and failed posts. Scheduling a post sets *when* it should go out;
actually publishing it currently requires either the scheduled time to
be reached by the platform's own background process or you clicking
"publish now" — ask your administrator whether automated scheduling is
enabled on this installation.

## Leads and sales

**Leads** is your pipeline — a kanban-style board across 7 stages (New
Lead through Won/Lost). Every lead gets a real, transparent score based
on genuine signals (product interest, disclosed budget, engagement) —
click into a lead to see exactly why it scored the way it did.
**Leads → Analytics** shows your real sales numbers (conversion rate,
revenue, ROAS, cost per sale) and lets you ask the AI sales agent
plain-language questions about your own pipeline — its answers are
always grounded in your real recorded data, never invented.

## Analytics

**Analytics** is your unified performance dashboard across every
connected platform. A dash (—) means that metric genuinely hasn't been
measured yet, not zero — the app never shows you a fabricated number.

## Optimization

**Optimization** lets you review AI-generated suggestions for
improving a campaign's performance, each with the real evidence behind
it. You choose the automation level per campaign:

- **Manual** — you review and decide every time
- **Assisted** — the AI still asks for your approval, but with a
  pre-filled recommendation
- **Autonomous** — the AI can act within limits you set (a daily
  spending cap, a maximum budget increase percentage, a maximum number
  of actions per day) — and you can hit **Emergency Stop** on any
  campaign at any time to immediately halt automated changes to it.

## The Orchestrator — your AI marketing team

**Orchestrator** is the most powerful feature: describe a real goal in
plain language — "help me get 100 sales for my product" — and the
system plans out the steps (research, strategy, content, advertising,
monitoring) and works through them one at a time. Anything involving
spending money, touching your ad account, or publishing content
**always pauses for your explicit approval** before it happens — this
is a structural guarantee, not a setting you could accidentally turn
off. Watch the **Activity timeline** on any run to see exactly what
each step is doing, why, what data it used, and what it recommends.

## AI Tools

A simpler, single-question interface if you don't need a whole
orchestrated run — ask one of the individual specialist agents (e.g.
the Marketing Strategy Agent) a direct question and get a
recommendation.

## Billing

**Billing** shows your current plan, your real usage this month across
every limited category (AI usage, campaigns, connected accounts,
content generated, automated actions), and lets you compare and switch
plans. Only your organization's owner can change plans.

## Team

Invite existing users to your organization and assign them a role
(Owner, Admin, Manager, Analyst, Content Manager, or Viewer) — each
role has different real permissions; ask your Owner if you're missing
access to something you expect to see.
